YOURoom modifications (2026-10-24):
- Native Kotlin/Compose rendering instead of Chromium's TypeScript browser runtime.
- Local viewport scaling, pause/resume and time-based simulation integration.
- Apply theme colors to the original gray/white sprite palette while rendering.
- Use upstream running/crashed sprites, obstacle collision definitions, pixel score
  glyphs, GAME OVER/restart sprites, cloud and two horizon strips.
- Adapt progressive speed and obstacle gaps for the mobile planning panel.
- Highest completed score is session-only; no persistent score, growth or network.
- No game sound, browser UI, Google logo display, automatic theme/night switch,
  online leaderboard, or claim of Google endorsement.

Game resources are bundled offline. Calculation completion remains a separate,
non-interrupting notification. No Chromium JavaScript/WebView runtime is embedded.
